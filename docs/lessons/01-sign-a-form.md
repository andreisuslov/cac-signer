# Sign a form

1. Say: CAC Signer signs PDF forms with the certificate on your CAC. Insert your card, then open the app.
   Screen: point "Drop a PDF here"
2. Say: Drop the PDF on the window, or press Choose PDF and pick it.
   Screen: choose "Training Request.pdf"
3. Say: The pages show on the left, and the form's signature fields are listed on the right.
   Screen: look
4. Say: The first empty field is picked for you. To sign another one, click it in the list or on the page.
   Screen: scroll 1 520; point ".box.existing.selected"; point "Requester Signature"
5. Say: The Digital Signature certificate from your card is already chosen.
   Screen: point "#identity"
6. Say: Press Sign, and choose where to save the signed copy. The original file stays as it was.
   Screen: saveas "Training Request signed.pdf"; click "Sign…"
7. Say: macOS asks for your PIN in its own window. Type it there. CAC Signer never sees it.
   Screen: finish; picture "signed"
8. Say: Done. The field shows a check mark, and your signature is on the page.
   Screen: point "✓ Signed by DOE.JANE.Q.1234567890"
9. Say: Show in Finder opens the folder with the signed copy, ready to send.
   Screen: point "Show in Finder"
